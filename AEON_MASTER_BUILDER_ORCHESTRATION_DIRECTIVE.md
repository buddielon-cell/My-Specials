---

3. EXISTING SYSTEM PRESERVATION

Existing AEON functionality must be treated as potentially valuable.

Do not delete, overwrite, or replace existing components merely because a newer design appears preferable.

For every proposed modification, determine:

1. What currently exists?
2. What does it do?
3. What depends on it?
4. What would replace it?
5. What would be gained?
6. What could be lost?
7. Can the existing functionality be extended instead?
8. Is migration necessary?
9. Is rollback possible?

Preserve backwards compatibility wherever reasonably possible.

8. SKEPTICAL ARCHITECT

Every consequential proposal must be evaluated by a dedicated Skeptical Architect.

The Skeptical Architect must actively attempt to invalidate the proposal.

It must ask:

- Is the problem genuine?
- Is the proposed solution necessary?
- Is there a simpler solution?
- Is the evidence sufficient?
- Are assumptions hidden?
- Is the capability exaggerated?
- Is there contradictory evidence?
- Could the change damage existing functionality?
- Could it introduce a security vulnerability?
- Could it increase unintended autonomy?
- Could it create excessive dependency?
- Can it be independently reproduced?
- Can it be rolled back?
- Is the benefit measurable?

A proposal that fails review shall enter:

REFINEMENT_REQUIRED

rather than being silently accepted.

19. AIRS RESEARCH LABORATORIES

Implement the following laboratories.

LAB 1 — Capability Discovery
Evaluate: reasoning, mathematics, coding, scientific reasoning, planning, tool use, function calling, memory, long context, instruction following, creativity, multilingual ability, self-correction, reflection, autonomous planning, workflow orchestration, simulation, knowledge synthesis.

LAB 2 — Benchmark Laboratory
Support: coding, reasoning, language, mathematical, knowledge, vision, audio, agent, workflow benchmarks.
Generate: scores, rankings, percentiles, trends, comparisons, historical progression.

LAB 3 — Prompt Research
Support: A/B testing, prompt tournaments, prompt mutation, prompt inheritance, prompt optimization, versioning, scoring, reproducibility.

LAB 4 — Agent Research
Evaluate: planning, decision quality, collaboration, completion, adaptability, failure recovery, tool selection, memory use, reliability.

LAB 5 — Workflow Research
Evaluate: success, latency, reliability, efficiency, scalability, failure recovery, robustness.

LAB 6 — Red Team
Test: hallucinations, contradictions, prompt injection, adversarial prompts, unsafe outputs, boundary conditions, ambiguity, long-context degradation, reasoning failures.

LAB 7 — Emergence Observatory
Investigate unexpected behaviours.
Do not classify a single unusual result as emergent capability.
Require repeated observation and reproducibility.

LAB 8 — Simulation Laboratory
Support controlled simulated environments including: research institutions, software organizations, scientific laboratories, educational environments, infrastructure, markets, cities, space missions.
Simulations must remain isolated from real-world consequential systems unless separately authorized.

LAB 9 — Evolution Laboratory
Track: model versions, agent versions, prompt versions, workflow versions, reasoning strategies.
Detect: improvements, regressions, capability changes.

LAB 10 — Analytics Laboratory
Provide dashboards for: capability radar, benchmark scores, latency, reliability, efficiency, cost, confidence, failure rate, trends, historical progression.
Support export to JSON, CSV, Markdown, HTML, PDF.

LAB 11 — Research Archive
Store complete experiment histories including configuration, inputs, outputs, metrics, evidence.

LAB 12 — Capability Registry
Registry containing name, description, evidence, confidence, maturity, validation status, limitations.

LAB 13 — Experiment Orchestrator
Support sequential, parallel, benchmark campaigns, parameter sweeps, repeated trials, dependencies.

LAB 14 — Scientific Method Engine
Every formal experiment follows observation to hypothesis to execution to reproduction to validation to conclusion.

LAB 15 — Research Publication Engine
Generate comprehensive results and summary documentation.

LAB 16 — Capability Reproduction Laboratory
Attempt independent reproduction of significant findings.

LAB 17 — Capability Transfer Laboratory
Determine whether a capability survives changes in model, prompt, environment, task, tools, context.
